    About

    One marque, done properly

    Desmo Shed is an independent Ducati specialist tucked behind Greenhill Parade in New Barnet. Annual, belt, desmo service or total engine rebuild — we cover all aspects, and we use only genuine Ducati OEM parts.

    We hold a full Ducati tooling kit and the latest tuning software, so the jobs a general workshop turns away are the ones we do every week: belt and desmo services, valve shimming, clutch and suspension work, track preparation and modifications.

    We're London's only Rexxer custom software authorised dealer, an authorised Bike Trac dealer and an official Motul lubricant stockist, and we fit MUPO race suspension and STM clutches.
